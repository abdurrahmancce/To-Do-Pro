SHARED TASKS - manual rules test (5 minutes, two accounts)
Use two accounts that are friends: A (owner) and B (member). Use two browsers or one normal and one private window.

1. Publish the new firestore.rules (Firebase Console > Firestore > Rules > paste > Publish).
2. A: Share sheet > People > add B (Can edit) > Share. Expect: the task shows "Shared 1" on A.
3. B: open "Shared with me". Expect: the task is listed. Tick a step, add a step, edit the title. Expect: A sees the change in a few seconds, and the activity log names B.
4. A: change B to "View only" (Share > People > Save changes). B: reopen the task. Expect: no title box, no tick boxes, no add-step box.
5. A: add a reminder 2 minutes ahead. Expect: B's phone/app rings too (B must have opened the app once after the share was created).
6. B: Leave this task. Expect: it disappears from B, A still has it.
7. A: Stop sharing. Expect: the "Shared" chip disappears.
8. Group board: A: Account > Friends and groups > open a group > Open group tasks > New group task (assign to B). Expect: B sees it under Shared with me. Tick it done: progress shows "1 of 1".

Rules that must REFUSE (open the browser console, or just trust the checklist above):
- A third account C (not a member) must not see the task (shares list stays empty for C).
- B with "View only" must get "permission denied" when it tries to change the task.
- B must not be able to add other people to the share.
